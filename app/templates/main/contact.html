FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do ApnaVision scooters go?

Our Commuter series reaches 15-20 mph, perfectly legal for most city bike lanes. The Performance and Off-Road series can reach up to 45 mph for private property use.

How long does shipping take?

All scooters ship via freight and generally arrive within 3-5 business days for continental US orders. International shipping can take 7-14 days.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes! Every ApnaVision comes with a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering the battery, motor, and controller against any manufacturing defects.
